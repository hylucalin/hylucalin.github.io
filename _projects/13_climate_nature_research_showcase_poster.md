On **26 June 2026**, I will present this academic poster at the **Climate and Nature Research Showcase**.

The poster summarises my fourth-year project, **Drone-borne Retrieval of Cloud Droplet Size Distributions Using Polarimetric Imagery**. The project develops a low-cost drone-borne method for estimating cloud droplet size distributions from polarised cloudbow-region imagery, with field data collected above valley fog in the Yorkshire Dales National Park.

The work connects marine cloud brightening measurement needs, polarisation camera calibration, anti-solar-point tracking, lookup-table fitting, and drone payload design. In the poster results, single-frame and multi-frame retrievals give consistent droplet size distributions, with effective radius around 5 micrometres and effective variance around 0.05. The multi-frame retrieval reaches approximately 3 m by 3 m spatial resolution.
