On **26 June 2026**, I will present an academic poster at the **Climate and Nature Research Showcase**.

The poster gives a compact overview of my fourth-year project, **Drone-borne Retrieval of Cloud Droplet Size Distributions Using Polarimetric Imagery**. The project uses a small drone, a polarisation camera, and lookup-table fitting to estimate cloud droplet size distributions from cloudbow-region imagery. The field data come from valley fog in the Yorkshire Dales National Park, where the retrieval produced effective droplet radii around 5 micrometres and multi-frame spatial resolution of about 3 m by 3 m.
