For Dyson Day, I introduced the core idea behind my fourth-year project: using a drone, polarised light, and image processing to estimate the size distribution of tiny droplets inside cloud or fog. The project asks whether a lightweight flying instrument can recover useful cloud microphysics from a faint optical feature called the cloudbow.

Read the school-level case study here: [Measuring Cloud Droplets From a Drone]({{ '/projects/12_drone_cloud_droplet_measurement/' | relative_url }}).
